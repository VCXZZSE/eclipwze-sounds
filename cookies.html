01 / Cookies and similar storage

Cookies are small pieces of information stored by a website on a device. Local storage and session storage are different browser mechanisms that can also remember information. We describe both here rather than using “cookies” to hide the difference.

In this version, the site’s own preference and contact-draft code uses local or session storage, not JavaScript cookies. Hosting/security infrastructure may use service-related technologies. YouTube can use cookies or device storage when you choose to load its player. We do not install advertising pixels.

02 / Your controls

Before you choose, optional categories are off. The banner offers equally accessible Reject optional, Accept optional and Settings controls. Continuing to scroll is not consent. Closing the settings dialog without saving does not change your choice.

04 / Optional measurements

Vercel Web Analytics uses aggregated data and a short-lived request-derived visitor hash rather than an advertising cookie. Speed Insights measures loading performance. These scripts remain off unless you allow Analytics & performance. This is an additional choice we provide; “cookieless” does not mean no information is processed.

Withdrawing this category saves your preference and reloads the page, because removing a script element alone cannot reliably stop code already running. It cannot retract measurement events already sent. Learn more in Vercel’s Web Analytics and Speed Insights privacy documentation.

05 / Optional YouTube embeds

A play button first opens a local privacy placeholder if YouTube is disabled. Load this video only gives permission for that player without remembering a media preference. Allow YouTube videos remembers the category so later players can load directly. Both choices tell you that your device connects to Google.

We use YouTube’s privacy-enhanced embed domain, youtube-nocookie.com. This limits certain personalization; it does not make the player completely free of data processing or guarantee no device storage. An ordinary “Open on YouTube” link remains available without enabling embedded media. Once you follow it, Google’s own controls apply.

06 / Clearing, signals and contact

Cookie settings includes a control to clear this site’s saved animation preferences and contact draft. Your consent decision is retained to respect it. To reset that decision as well, delete site data in your browser. Browser settings can remove third-party cookies that this site cannot access.

We do not sell data or share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. Optional services are off by default, including when no affirmative choice has been made; an opt-out signal is never treated as an opt-in. We do not use a fingerprint-based workaround when storage is blocked.
