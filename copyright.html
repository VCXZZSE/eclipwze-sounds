01 / What the notice means

The Eclipwze Sounds name, original site text, commissioned or original artwork and band performances are protected only to the extent the band or the relevant contributor holds those rights. Third-party names, trademarks, images, compositions, lyrics and recordings belong to their respective owners.

Where we credit a cover or a medley, that identifies the performance; it does not claim ownership of the original composition. A recording can have different owners and permissions from the song it contains. Neither this page nor a “no infringement intended” statement supplies a missing licence or prevents a legitimate claim.

02 / Listening and sharing

You can share links to our public pages and use the available platform players in accordance with the law and platform terms. Website access does not grant permission to download, re-upload, sample, remix, use music in a video or advert, train a model on protected material, or reproduce artwork, except where a licence or a legal exception permits the particular use.

Permission for one platform or use should not be assumed to cover another. Covers, arrangements, medleys, audiovisual synchronization, samples and promotion can involve different permissions. Whether a work is public domain can also depend on the country and the particular arrangement or recording.

03 / Request permission or correct a credit

Email eclipwze74@gmail.com with the subject “Permission / credits”. Tell us which material you mean, its page or video link, the intended use, platforms, countries, duration and whether the use is commercial. We can discuss rights we control and identify when additional rightsholder permission is needed. Please wait for written permission before proceeding where permission is required.

For a credit correction, include the current credit, the correction and supporting information. We may ask a contributor or rightsholder to help verify a disputed credit.

04 / Raise a rights concern

If you believe material on this website infringes a right you own or represent, email eclipwze74@gmail.com with “Rights concern” in the subject and include:

  • Your name and a reliable contact address, and whether you are the rightsholder or an authorised representative.
  • The work or right concerned and enough information to identify it.
  • The exact website URLs or video links at issue and a clear description of the concern.
  • The basis of your ownership or authority, and supporting information reasonably needed to assess the issue.
  • The action you are requesting, such as correcting a credit, removing an image or disabling a link.

Do not send unnecessary identity documents or sensitive personal information. We review specific concerns, may request clarification and, where appropriate, correct, restrict or remove disputed material while the matter is assessed. We do not promise a fixed response time or a particular outcome before reviewing the facts. For material hosted on YouTube or another provider, that provider also has its own rights-reporting procedure.

This email route is a practical contact process; it is not a statement that we have appointed a U.S. DMCA agent or qualified for a safe harbour. Applicable formal notice requirements and legal rights are unaffected.
