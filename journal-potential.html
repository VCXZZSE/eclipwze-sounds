Unlock Your Musical Potential with Eclipwze Sounds

BY ECLIPWZE7414 June 2024 · 2 min read · Updated 18 June 2024
FROM OUR SIDE
OF THE MUSIC.04The full archive ↗

Are you looking to unlock your musical potential and take your passion for music to the next level? Look no further than Eclipwze Sounds, a dynamic music band dedicates to providing top-notch collaboration to music enthusiasts like you.

Are you a rising artist? or maybe an aspiring artist who has got skills and finds himself/herself really connected with music. Worry not, we have got you covered. Before starting, we assume that you already know our story. If not, then you may consider visiting our About Us page or Our Story blog posted in the website first.

Why Collaborate with us?

If you are a talented or aspiring singer/musician looking to enhance your skills through collaboration and building a reputation, Eclipwze Sounds is a great choice for you. We have successfully worked with videographers and vocalists in the past, resulting in outstanding music videos and songs every time. We are currently present on over all major music streaming platforms and gratefully welcome people to join us by providing music ideas/ music collaborations/ some other ways. The reason behind choosing us is very simple. You get to experience how tricky and interesting recording a music can get. With a chance to feature your self on leading music streaming platforms. Collaborating at an early age can drastically improve your sense to understand music and help you to get an opportunity to interact with other musicians.

Eligibilty Criteria:

We understand collaborating with other musicians could be tricky but Eclipwze Sounds, takes great pride in announcing that we are always there to help newcomers or firsttimer's out 24x7. The eligibility to collaborate with us are :

  1. One has to be a music lover/connected to music/have a music background.
  2. One could be a musician/vocalist.
  3. Has something to offer and brings skills to the table.
  4. Musician/Vocalist must have experience of minimum 2 years depending on the genre (Western/Classical).
  5. The criteria could be over looked if one has adequate skills and knowledge about music.

How To Collaborate?

It's important for enthusiasts to understand that they won't receive any money. However, based on their skills and the instruments they play or the videos they record, they will be rewarded accordingly. The rewards will be so enticing that they will be impossible to resist.

Click on the link attached to the Form, you would be re-directed to our Google Form page where you can pitch us your idea or maybe send us a formal request for collaboration with a brief description of things you can offer. You would be attended by our team within 48 working hours. And if found relevant, would be given further updates.
