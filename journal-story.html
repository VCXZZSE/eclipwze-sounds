Eclipwze Sounds: Our Journey

OF THE MUSIC.02The full archive ↗

This is the story of how Eclipwze Sounds was built from scratch by two music freaks — and how we’re still finding our way!

REPOME & DEB BANERJEE

Eclipwze Sounds was just an idea when we were in high school. My partner Deb and I were bench mates, and we were both deeply enthusiastic about music. Whenever we had a free moment, we found ways to entertain ourselves and our classmates by singing, coming up with mashups, or trying out ideas for remixes. We were in the school band and spent most of our free time in the auditorium or activity room, trying to stay close to music in any way we could. Deb played the guitar, and I played the violin.

The idea of starting a band with our friends and the juniors in our school band was always there. It began to take shape when Deb and I opened our YouTube channel and posted some violin covers. The first video wasn’t a success. But as we gradually uploaded new songs every month, the channel began to take flight. At the time of writing, it had received more than 5,000 views on YouTube.

Eventually, we started distributing our music on major streaming platforms, including Spotify, Apple Music, JioSaavn, SoundCloud, and even Amazon Music! Unfortunately, we had to begin without our friends. But we still believe that, someday, we’ll collaborate with them — and this band will truly be “OUR BAND”!

As we continue sharing our melodies and performances, we hope to make our mark on the music industry. With our blend of talent, creativity, and passion, this journey is only just beginning.
