This is the story of how Eclipse Sounds was built from scratch by two music freaks and is still working its way to become established!

REPOME & DEB BANERJEE

Eclipwze Sounds was just an idea when we were in high school. Me and my partner Deb were bench mates and were very enthusiastic towards music. Whenever we used to get time, we would always find ways to entertain ourselves and our fellow classmates by singing songs or maybe creating some attractive ideas to mashup or remix songs. We were in the school band and would usually prefer to spend most of our times in the school auditorium or the activity room trying to stay attached with music at any cost. Not to mention, Deb was a guitarist and I used to play the violin.

The idea of starting a band together with our friends and our juniors who were a part of the school band was always there. But it took shape when I and Deb decided to open our Youtube channel and post some cover songs which I played on the violin. The first video was not a success but as we gradually started to upload new songs every month, the channel started to take a flight and currently has more than 5000 views on Youtube. Eventually, we even started distributing our music over major music streaming platforms like Spotify, Apple Music, JioSaavn, Soundcloud & even Amazon Music! Unfortunately, we had to start without our friends but we believe eventually we would someday, collaborate with them and this band would be called "

OUR BAND

" !

As we continue to enchant audiences with our enchanting melodies and soulful performances, Eclipwze Sounds is poised to make an indelible mark on the music industry. With our unique blend of talent, creativity, and passion, they are truly a force to be reckoned with, and their journey is only just beginning.
