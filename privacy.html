01 / Who is responsible?

This notice covers the official Eclipwze Sounds website at eclipwze-sounds.vercel.app. Eclipwze Sounds is the Kolkata, India music duo run by Repome Roy Mondal and Deb Banerjee. We are responsible for the personal information we handle for this website and our correspondence. For privacy requests, contact eclipwze74@gmail.com with “Privacy request” in the subject.

02 / What information is involved?

  • Contact information you choose to provide: your name, email address, query type and message, plus an optional phone number.
  • A draft in your browser: the form prepares your email locally. When you request a Gmail or email-app draft, this site keeps a copy in this tab’s session storage so you can return to it. The form does not submit your message to a site backend.
  • Email correspondence: when you send the email yourself, we receive the message and associated email metadata in our Gmail inbox.
  • Delivery and security: our hosting provider Vercel processes requests, which can include your IP address, browser details, requested pages and security logs, to serve and protect the website.
  • Optional measurements: if enabled, Vercel Web Analytics and Speed Insights collect aggregated visit and performance information such as pages, referrers, approximate location, device/browser type and loading metrics. We do not send contact-form content to these tools.
  • Optional external content: loading a YouTube player connects your device to Google. Browser information and device storage may be involved. Following external social, streaming or form links takes you to those providers.

03 / Why we use information

We use correspondence to answer questions, discuss performances and collaborations, manage permissions and keep records needed for those conversations. We use necessary technical information to deliver the site and address abuse or security issues. Optional measurements help us understand visits and improve loading speed.

Where a legal basis is required, we rely on consent for optional services; steps you request before a booking or collaboration agreement, or performing that agreement, where relevant; legitimate interests in responding to general correspondence and protecting our site, subject to your rights; and legal obligations where applicable. We do not use consent to optional cookies as a condition for reading the site or contacting us.

04 / Providers and international access

The relevant providers are Vercel for hosting and optional measurements, and Google for Gmail and optional YouTube embeds. Third-party streaming, social and Google Forms links operate under their own policies after you choose to visit them.

We work from India. Providers may process information in other countries, whose laws may differ from yours. Their privacy notices describe their international processing and safeguards. Where applicable law requires us to arrange a particular transfer safeguard for information we disclose, that requirement still applies.

We do not sell personal information or share it for targeted advertising. This website does not install advertising trackers, run automated eligibility decisions, or profile visitors for marketing. We do not add an enquiry to a mailing list.

05 / How long information remains

Your consent choice is stored on your browser for up to 180 days before it is requested again. Optional opening-screen memory lasts for the tab session; optional duo preferences remain until you withdraw preference storage or clear local data. The contact draft remains in session storage until the tab session ends or you clear it through Cookie settings or the form.

Emails remain in our inbox only as long as needed for the enquiry, any resulting relationship, rights records or applicable legal obligations. The period depends on the conversation and the record’s purpose; there is no automatic website inbox-deletion timer. Provider security logs, measurements, backups and email systems have their own retention settings. Ask us about a particular record or request deletion when it is no longer needed.

06 / Your choices and rights

You can refuse all optional services, change individual categories, or withdraw consent using at any time. Withdrawal applies going forward and does not undo processing that already occurred. Disabling analytics reloads this page to stop its loaded scripts. Disabling media removes active embedded players. The site cannot delete cookies that Google has already placed on its own domains; use your browser or Google controls for those.

Depending on the law that applies, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, an explanation of processing, or object to certain processing. You may withdraw consent without affecting earlier lawful processing. Write to eclipwze74@gmail.com; we may request proportionate information to verify your request. We will respond within the period required by applicable law and explain any applicable exception. You can also complain to the data-protection authority responsible for your location. Nothing in this notice limits mandatory rights.

07 / Security, younger visitors and updates

We limit handling to what is needed for the site and correspondence, but no website, email system or transmission is completely secure. Please do not include passwords, financial credentials or sensitive identity documents in an enquiry.

The site is a general music site and is not designed to collect children’s information. If you are below the age at which you can lawfully consent to a particular service, ask a parent or guardian before sharing personal information or enabling it. A parent or guardian can contact eclipwze74@gmail.com about a child’s information.

We will update this notice when our services or practices change. A materially different optional use will require a new consent choice rather than silently extending an old one.
